INTEGRATED SAFETY & COMPLIANCE SOLUTIONS.

Structured consulting, protective equipment supply, and corporate portal registrations tailored for mining, construction, and industrial contractors.

01 / Division

HEALTH & SAFETY

Comprehensive workplace safety advisory, statutory file compilation, site inspections, and SHEQ compliance management.

  • Strategic advisory to align workplace operations with South African occupational health and safety expectations.

  • Site-specific, structured contractor safety files prepared for mining, construction, and industrial client onboarding.

  • Baseline and issue-based HIRA documentation identifying operational hazards and practical mitigation controls.

  • Systematic compliance reviews and corrective action reporting to maintain operational readiness.

  • Workforce safety briefings, induction support, and hazard awareness sessions to cultivate a proactive safety culture.

  • Integrated Safety, Health, Environment, and Quality frameworks designed for sustainable operational governance.

  • Dedicated project safety oversight, contractor documentation control, and site risk governance.

  • On-site physical walkthroughs verifying equipment, signage, PPE usage, and safe working procedures.
